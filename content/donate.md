### Why Your Gift Matters

Pulse of Life is a small, tuition-free kindergarten in Dara'a, Syria, sustained entirely by people who believe that children affected by displacement deserve more than survival. Every gift goes directly toward keeping our classrooms open — teachers, Montessori materials, warm meals, and the small practical things a family needs to send a child to school.
